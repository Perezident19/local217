IATSE Local 217 | Rockford, Illinois

The work behind the show starts here.

Local 217 is made up of stagehands and technicians who do the work behind live events in the Rockford region. We unload trucks, build stages, run cable, rig shows, move road cases, solve problems, and work together to get the job done safely.

We have been doing this work for years, and what matters to us is showing up ready, respecting the work, and taking care of the crew around you.

What We Do

Most people see the show. We are what it takes to build it.

Live events take a lot of people doing different jobs at the right time. Local 217 members work behind the scenes on concerts, theater productions, arena events, casino entertainment, festivals, touring productions, sporting events, and special events.

Training & Apprenticeship

There is always more to learn.

Stagehand work takes skill, preparation, and experience. Training gives newer hands a place to learn the basics and gives experienced hands a chance to keep building on what they know.

Local 217 training includes orientation, hands-on learning, leadership and steward education, and opportunities through the IATSE Training Trust Fund.

Get Involved

There's more to the Local than the call.

There are plenty of ways to take part beyond the work itself. Training, meetings, committees, events, and other Local work all need members willing to help.

You don't have to do everything. Find where you can help, learn how that part of the Local works, and go from there.
