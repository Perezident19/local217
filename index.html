IATSE Local 217 | Rockford, Illinois

Skilled hands behind live events.

IATSE Local 217 represents stagehands and entertainment workers who help make live events happen across the Rockford region. Our members work behind the scenes in arenas, theaters, concerts, casino entertainment, festivals, sporting events, touring productions, and special events.

What matters most

Safe work, fair treatment, skilled labor, training the next generation, respect on the job, solidarity, and doing the work right.

Safe work and fair treatment

We believe every worker deserves a jobsite where safety matters, questions are respected, and pressure never overrides doing the work right.

Skilled labor and training

We build stronger crews by helping new workers learn the basics and giving experienced members more chances to sharpen their skills.

Respect and solidarity

Local 217 is built by members who show up, teach the next person, respect the crew, and protect standards on the job.
