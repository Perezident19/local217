IATSE Local 217 | Rockford, Illinois

The work behind the show starts here.

Local 217 is made up of stagehands and technicians who do the work behind live events in the Rockford region. We unload trucks, build stages, run cable, rig shows, move road cases, solve problems, and work together to get the job done safely.

What we do

Most people see the show. We are what it takes to build it.

Behind the scenes

Local 217 members work behind the scenes on concerts, theater productions, arena events, casino entertainment, festivals, touring productions, sporting events, and special events.

Training matters

Stagehand work takes skill, preparation, and experience. Training gives newer hands a place to learn the basics and experienced hands a chance to keep building on what they know.

More than the call

There are plenty of ways to take part beyond the work itself. Training, meetings, committees, events, and other Local work all need members willing to help.
