Training & Apprenticeship

Training is part of the work.

Nobody learns this trade all at once. Some things are taught in a class. A lot is learned by working alongside people who have done it before, asking questions, and getting more experience over time.

Local 217 training gives members, apprentices, and extras a place to build skills, understand the Local, and get better prepared for the work ahead.

Stagehand Orientation

Orientation gives newer hands a chance to start learning the expectations of the work before everything is moving around them.

That includes safety, communication, basic work practices, asking questions when you do not know something, and understanding what people expect from you on a call.

Hands-On Training

Some parts of the trade have to be learned by doing them. Hands-on classes give people time to practice, ask questions, and learn from experienced hands without the pace of a live production.

Classes can cover different parts of the work depending on what people need to learn and what the Local is working on.

Leadership & Steward Basics

Knowing the physical work is only part of understanding the Local.

Leadership and steward training covers Local structure, documentation, safety, communication, member involvement, and how concerns move through the right channels. The goal is to give more people a better understanding of how the Local works and what responsibility looks like when they step into it.

IATSE Training Trust Fund

Members may also have access to training through the IATSE Training Trust Fund.

TTF classes use a separate registration process and may have eligibility requirements. Check the official class information before registering.

Growing in the Local

There is a path through the Local, but it is not something that happens overnight.

Extras can begin learning the work and the expectations around it. Apprentices continue building experience, training, and understanding of the trade. Membership comes with greater responsibility to the work, the standards, and the Local.

Training is one part of that process. So are showing up, listening, learning, and gaining experience over time.
