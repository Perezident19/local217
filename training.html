Training & Apprenticeship

Training is part of the work.

Nobody learns this trade all at once. Some things are taught in a class. A lot is learned by working alongside people who have done it before, asking questions, and getting more experience over time.

Training & Apprenticeship

Local 217 training gives members, apprentices, and extras a place to build skills, understand the Local, and get better prepared for the work ahead.

Stagehand orientation

Orientation gives newer hands a chance to start learning safety, communication, basic work practices, asking questions, and what people expect on a call.

Hands-on training

Some parts of the trade have to be learned by doing them. Hands-on classes give people time to practice, ask questions, and learn from experienced hands.

IATSE Training Trust Fund

Members may also have access to training through the IATSE Training Trust Fund. TTF classes use a separate registration process and may have eligibility requirements.

Leadership & steward basics

Leadership and steward training covers Local structure, documentation, safety, communication, member involvement, and how concerns move through the right channels.

Growing in the Local

Extras can begin learning the work and expectations. Apprentices continue building experience. Membership comes with greater responsibility to the work, the standards, and the Local.

Upcoming training

Local 217 training and IATSE Training Trust Fund opportunities. Keep changing dates in the live training calendar as details are confirmed.
