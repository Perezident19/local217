Interested in live event work?

You do not need to know everything before you reach out. This is the first step in letting Local 217 know who you are and how to contact you.

What to expect

IATSE Local 217 is always looking for dependable people who are willing to learn, show up, work safely, and be part of a crew.

Stagehand work covers a lot: loading trucks, pushing cases, setting rooms, building stages, running cable, assisting departments, and learning the rhythm of live events.

Filling out this form does not guarantee work or membership, but it helps Local 217 learn who you are, what experience you have, and how to contact you when opportunities or training come up.

Interest form submissions should go to letstrain217@gmail.com once connected.
