Interested in live event work?

Stagehand work is hands-on, physical, technical, and different from one call to the next. You might be unloading trucks, moving road cases, building staging, running cable, helping a department get set, or working a load-out after the show.

What matters when you are new

Nobody expects perfection on a first call, but people remember whether you listened, took correction well, helped the crew, and gave an honest effort.

What matters is being honest about what you know. If you know how to do something, say so. If you do not, ask. We can teach somebody who is willing to learn. We cannot work safely around somebody pretending to know something they do not.

Show up when you are supposed to. Pay attention. Stay engaged. Treat people with respect. Take safety seriously.

Submitting the interest form is a first step. It does not guarantee work or membership.

Interest form submissions should go to letstrain217@gmail.com once connected.

Do I need tools?

You do not need to go buy a pile of tools before filling out an interest form. The form asks what tools you already have, including an option for "I do not have tools yet."

Transportation

Reliable transportation matters because calls can happen at different locations and times. Car access and a driver's license are helpful information, but they are not hard requirements for submitting the form.

A path forward

People can enter the Local's work as extras, continue developing as apprentices, and work toward membership over time through experience, training, and understanding the work.

What happens after you submit

A Local 217 representative can follow up with you. You will have a chance to learn more about expectations, safety basics, and available training.

You will also learn where to find upcoming training, meetings, and other Local information.

If you are interested, raise your hand. We will start there.
