Weekly member meeting

Members Meet Every Monday At 8:00 PM.

Regular meetings give us a place to handle Local business, ask questions, talk through what's going on, and keep people informed.

You don't have to come to a meeting with something to say. Sometimes showing up, listening, and learning how things work is enough. If you have a question, want to understand more about the Local, or are looking for a way to get involved, meetings are a good place to start.

Looking For Work-Call Information?

Work calls, call times, and show labor details aren't posted on the public calendar. Members should continue using CallSteward, email, and other private member communication for that information.

Looking For Training?

Training opportunities are included on the public calendar so members, apprentices, and extras can see what's coming up.

Local 217 classes and IATSE Training Trust Fund classes use different registration processes. If you're looking for class details, registration information, or more about how training works, you'll find it on the Training & Apprenticeship page.
