Membership Meetings

The Meeting Starts Before The Gavel

Membership meetings are where we handle Local business, but they're also one of the times people get to be around each other when we're not in the middle of a call.

People come in early, catch up, ask questions, and talk about what's going on. There's usually food or something to snack on before and after, but once the meeting starts, we handle the business of the Local.

You don't have to walk in with something to say. Showing up, listening, and getting to know the people around you matters too.
