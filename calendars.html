Weekly member meeting

Members meet every Monday at 8:00 PM.

Regular meetings give us a place to handle Local business, ask questions, talk through what is going on, and keep people informed.

You do not have to come to a meeting with something to say. Sometimes showing up, listening, and learning how things work is enough.

Weekly Member Meeting

Membership | 8:00 PM | Local 217 Union Hall

If you have a question, want to understand more about the Local, or are looking for a way to get involved, meetings are a good place to start.

Looking for work-call information?

Work calls, call times, and show labor details are not posted on the public calendar. Members should continue using CallSteward, email, and other private member communication for that information.

Looking for training?

Training opportunities are included on the public calendar so members, apprentices, and extras can see what is coming up. Local 217 classes and IATSE Training Trust Fund classes use different registration processes.
