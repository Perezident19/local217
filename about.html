About Local 217

A working local built by the people who show up.

Local 217 is a proud stagehand local rooted in safe work, fair treatment, strong skills, and respect on the job.

Who we are

Local 217 is a working local. Our strength comes from the people who show up, learn the craft, protect the standards, train the next person, and take care of the crew around them.

Whether someone has been doing this for thirty years or is walking into their first call, our goal is to build a local where workers know what is expected, know where to go for help, and have a real path to grow.

Local 217 mindset: train the bench, protect the work, respect the process, and build the future.

How to join

Start with a simple interest form.

Interested workers can fill out the interest form, share their experience level, and ask questions about training, expectations, and getting started.

Filling out the form does not guarantee work or membership, but it is the first step in letting Local 217 know who you are and how to contact you when opportunities or training come up.

Joining path

1. Submit the interest form.
2. Talk with a Local 217 representative.
3. Learn expectations, safety basics, and available training.
4. Stay connected through calendars, meetings, and calls.
